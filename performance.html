Performance

Throughput and latency

How fast Felix moves records, with the setup behind each number. These are single-broker results; replicated, multi-node results are coming and will be added here.

Single broker

Measured on one broker

Preview results from Felix 0.6.0 on one 8 vCPU Azure VM with local NVMe, driven by four load generators in the same region. "Durable" means the broker fsyncs before it acks. These are single-broker numbers, and replicated multi-node results are coming.

0.6.0-preview · single region · more results coming

4.1GB/s

Published, in memory

4 KiB records in batches of 64, fire-and-forget. The broker was CPU-bound at 7.4 of 8 cores. Mean of 3 runs.

1.43GB/s

Published, durable

Same workload with an fsync before each ack. 97% of what fio measured on the same NVMe array. Mean of 3 runs.

2.27M records/s

Acked, durable, small records

256 B records in batches of 64, fsync before each ack. 2.27 to 2.29 million across 3 runs.

345µs p50

Durable publish to delivery

One 256 B publish in flight, received by a subscriber on a second connection after the fsync. p99 445 µs. 2 runs.
